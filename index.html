Practical Based Assessment (PBA) — Federal Board

FBISE replaced the traditional practical notebook + viva system with the Practical Based Assessment model. The Composite HSSC paper has two sections — Section A (Major Practicals) and Section B (Minor Practicals) — sat as a single written paper based on procedures, data, and graph interpretation from experiments actually performed in the lab.

fbise.edu.pk — official model papers & frameworks

Paper Guidelines

i. The paper will consist of two sections, i.e. Section A and Section B.
ii. Section A includes Major Practicals. This section has three questions, each carrying 6 marks with parts, and each question is based on a single practical (performance / calculation / procedures / observations).
iii. Section B includes Minor Practicals. This section also has three questions, each carrying 4 marks with parts. Each question may be based on a single practical or on multiple practicals.
iv. The weightage of Section A is 60% (18 marks), while Section B is 40% (12 marks) — total 30 marks.
v. There are no marks for the practical notebook or viva voce — but students may record procedures, observations, apparatus, and calculations on any plain paper/worksheet/practical folder to aid revision.
vi. Performing every prescribed practical in the laboratory during the whole academic session is mandatory — only students who have done so may attempt the PBA.
vii. MCQs are not included/assessed in the PBA paper (the quizzes on this site are for self-practice only).
viii. Questions carrying 0.5 marks are not included/assessed as a single part in any section of the PBA paper.

Section A — Major Practicals 60% · 18 marks

  • 01 Measure the volume of a small spherical object using screw gauge and calculate the uncertainty in the result. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 02 Determine the radius of curvature of a convex lens and a concave lens using a spherometer. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 03 Investigate the fall of a body through a viscous medium and calculate the coefficient of viscosity of the fluid. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 04 Study stationary waves using Melde's apparatus and calculate the frequency of the AC source. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 05 Determine the wavelength of sound in air using stationary waves, and calculate the speed of sound at 0°C using a resonance tube. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 06 Compare the emf of cells using a potentiometer. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 07 Determine the time period, frequency, and angular frequency of a simple pendulum by varying its length. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 08 Determine the relation between current and capacitance for different combinations of capacitors in AC circuits. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 09 Study the charging and discharging of a capacitor through a resistor and calculate the time constant. ✓ Live apparatus on this site

Section B — Minor Practicals 40% · 12 marks

  • 01 Calculate elastic potential energy from the load-extension graph for a vertical mass-spring system. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 02 Sketch the magnetic field pattern due to currents in a long straight wire / a flat circular coil / a long solenoid. ✓ On this site
  • 03 Investigate the value of 'g' by free fall method using an electronic timer. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 04 Study the input and output waveforms for half-wave rectification using a CRO. (Questions will not be asked from CRO.) ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 05 Study the input and output waveforms for full-wave rectification using a CRO. (Questions will not be asked from CRO.) ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 06 Study the variation of electric current with intensity of light using a photoelectric apparatus. ✓ On this site

This reference reflects the Composite HSSC PBA scheme (Physics Curriculum 2022-23, Scheme of Studies 2006) for Annual Examination 2026 & onwards. Always cross-check against the current notice on the FBISE website before your exam, since frameworks are periodically revised.
