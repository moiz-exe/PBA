Practical Based Assessment (PBA) — Federal Board

FBISE replaced the traditional practical notebook + viva system with the Practical Based Assessment model. Every PBA paper has two sections — Section A drawn from Part-I of the practical list, and Section B drawn from Part-II — sat as a single written paper based on procedures, data, and graph interpretation from experiments actually performed in the lab.

fbise.edu.pk — official model papers & frameworks

Paper Guidelines Verbatim from FBISE notification

i. There are two sections in the PBA paper. Section A carries one question with multiple parts; Section B carries one question with multiple parts.
ii. In Section A, Question 1 is based on only one experiment taken from Part-I of the practical list.
iii. In Section B, Question 2 is based on multiple experiments taken from Part-II of the practical list.
iv. Weightage of Part-I practicals is 60%, while Part-II practicals carry 40% of the PBA paper.
v. Diagrams must be drawn wherever the question asks for one.
vi. There are no marks for the practical notebook or viva voce — but keeping a worksheet/practical folder of your own procedures, observations, and calculations is strongly recommended for revision.
vii. Performing every prescribed practical in the lab during the academic year is mandatory; only students who have done so may attempt the PBA paper.
viii. No MCQs are asked in the PBA paper itself (the quizzes on this site are for self-practice only).
ix. No 0.5-mark question is asked in either section of the PBA paper.

List of Physics Practicals — HSSC-II Part-I · 60%

  • 01 Determine resistance of a wire by slide wire (metre) bridge. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 02 Determine internal resistance of a cell using a potentiometer. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 03 Determine e.m.f. of a cell using a potentiometer. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 04 Determine the e.m.f. and internal resistance of a cell by plotting a V against I graph. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 05 Determine resistance of a voltmeter by drawing a graph between R and I/V.
  • 06 Determine the relation between current and capacitance for different parallel combinations of capacitors in an AC circuit.

List of Physics Practicals — HSSC-II Part-II · 40%

  • 01 Calculate elastic potential energy from the load-extension graph for a vertical mass-spring system. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 02 Sketch the magnetic field pattern due to currents in a long straight wire / a flat circular coil / a long solenoid. ✓ On this site
  • 03 Determine time constant by charging and discharging a capacitor through a resistor.
  • 04 Study the input and output waveforms for half-wave rectification using a CRO. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
  • 05 Study the input and output waveforms for full-wave rectification using a CRO. ✓ Live apparatus on this site
